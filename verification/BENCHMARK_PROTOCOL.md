# Independent cross-department benchmark protocol — NOT EXECUTED

Freeze the reviewer-approved employer source, whole JD and original CV documents **before** model tuning. Two qualified blinded reviewers label every `R###` as Found, Transferable, Not Found, employer-meaning review or technical coverage gap, with original `S###` evidence and missing conditions. Resolve disagreements with a separate adjudicator or retain ambiguity. Split held-out material by whole JD/employer and paraphrase family, not by sentences shared with developer fixtures. Include textile, finance, software QA, DevOps, customer service, logistics, HR, healthcare, and unseen occupations. Do not publish a hardcoded target score. Judge JD source coverage, full CV source/role coverage, per-R verdict + original citation entailment, score/rank parity and operational cost/retry behavior separately.

Compare old v51 and v52 in independent separate environments with pinned models, fixed date, equal budgets, approved meaning and source versions; record model/provider config, wall time, billed/estimated input/output tokens, HTTP transmissions and invalid schema counts. For local Ollama measure actual runtime; for OpenRouter obtain explicit budget and permitted CV transmission before any real API calls. Synthetic scripted 7/7 and 15/15 smoke checks are engineering controls **not** independent ML accuracy. Missing human labels and external provider access are **OPEN**, not passing benchmarks.
