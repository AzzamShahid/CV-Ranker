# Source-grounded decision contract (v50 current and future work)

One **supervisor-approved frozen original JD** applies to all candidates. Each `R###` refers to the same source wording, priority and reviewed meaning for a given revision. `RequirementConstraint.kind` describes the type; `evidence_modality` is separately stored to distinguish self-reported knowledge from performed work, dated professional tenure and completed credentials. The attached source-to-R diagnostic ledger is evidence coverage, not new scored requirements. A proposed meaning can still require supervisor review when the employer's wording is ambiguous.

## Evidence authority

1. Original candidate-specific segments `S###` + original exact wording define what the resume **claims**, not whether the external credential or job history is authentic.
2. Retrieval, YAKE hints, semantic similarity and generated restatements are **discovery only**.
3. LLM semantic decisions are **proposals**. Candidate-attributed performed work, negative/aspirational scope, source IDs, named alternatives, professional tenure, academic completion and mandatory JD conditions must survive independent application checks before credit.
4. Source validation distinguishes **retrieved**, **transmitted**, **model-cited**, **accepted**, **rejected** and **human-review** evidence. Review leads cannot be represented as accepted proof.
5. A grossly unanchored worked-duty claim cannot automatically earn direct Found. This rule deliberately blocks the example “reconcile ledger” versus “operate forklift”; more linguistically distant genuine synonyms can require further human/source equivalence review. A lexical anchor does not independently prove entailment.
6. Only the final verified status, and not retrieval/matched_term metadata or exploratory optional UI judges, may award configured points.

## Outcomes and scoring

| State | Source/technical condition | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| `Found` | Original candidate evidence supports all applicable reviewed mandatory conditions, at least one allowed OR option and shared duty/scope | Full configured criterion weight |
| `Transferable` | Actually completed related function, material named/direct gap stated and original evidence accepted | **0** direct points |
| `Not Found` | The relevant available original evidence was adequately assessed and does not support direct or meaningful related work | **0** |
| `Unverified` | Technical/model/source/coverage obstacle prevents a defensible decision, with typed reason and next action | **0 earned**, potential contributes to provisional ceiling |
| JD-review-needed | Employer meaning is ambiguous prior to candidate scoring | Not a candidate judgment |

A listed affirmative skill can support an **ordinary knowledge-only** requirement; it does not establish a completed deployment or quantified professional years. Numeric tenure uses source-linked dates and status, the immutable `assessment_as_of_date`, and no overlapping double-count. A completed relevant higher academic award may satisfy a general lower-degree minimum but never manufactures an explicitly separately required degree or license.

**Required + Preferred** items share the Requirements bucket by default, with an opt-in weighted split that divides that bucket only. Item/category weight changes recalculate without more AI requests. An incomplete candidate's provisional rank/score must remain visibly distinguishable from a fully verified rank.

## Scope boundary

This document describes policy and *partly implemented* v50 safety controls. It does **not** claim a fully centralized state machine across unified/fallback/optional focused paths, human acceptance of all semantic equivalences or full Windows/live-model release qualification. Refer to the implementation ledger for verified and outstanding work.
