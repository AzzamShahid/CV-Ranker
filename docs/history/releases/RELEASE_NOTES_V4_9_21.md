# V4.9.21 — Qualitative work evidence and a quieter supervisor interface

This update preserves the V4.9.20 reviewer-approved JD gate, final decision
labels, score denominator, negation checks, and cited-source export contract.

## Evidence and schema

- Distinguishes `experience_mode=tenure` (explicit numeric minimum-years JD
  rule) from `experience_mode=qualitative` (completed work without a new
  calendar gate). Priority (`required` or `preferred`) stays independent.
- The initial evidence context now gives dated role headers and their own
  performed duties priority for tenure requests, and completed work for
  qualitative deliverable requests.
- Source-backed Found evidence already cited for one requirement can be shown
  as an independent review lead for overlapping requirements; no scoring
  result is copied across criteria.
- The existing, bounded independent semantic review can revisit a potentially
  overlooked qualitative experience result. This is not automatic acceptance.
- Clarifies inclusive OR requirements and the distinction between
  `deployable` and `already deployed` in the model instructions.
- Keeps original source citations, negation safeguards, role duration checks,
  and tests for unrelated work and OR-skill alternatives.

## Interface

- Sidebar keeps provider, model identity and advanced settings without lengthy
  on-screen explanations.
- Analyze focuses on upload, review/approval and assessment, with optional
  samples and cloud recovery tucked away.
- Results retain one decision table, readable drill-down evidence, candidate
  counts, warnings, and CSV/JSON exports, with repetitive explanations removed.
- CV source ledger remains searchable in one table; a single inspector provides
  exact source lines for a selected segment.
- Technical details group execution, JD/YAKE information and downloads.

## Evaluation caveat

CV statements are self-reported. New evidence leads are not direct scoring
credit until validated against the frozen JD and the original CV.
